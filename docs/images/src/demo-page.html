The Lighthouse Loop

Cliff paths, two quiet coves and a lighthouse at the far end. Start before sunrise and you will have the trail to yourself.

12 km4 hoursEasyLoop
12.4 kmTotal distance
180 mElevation gain
5Stops worth the pause
05:40Best start time

Five stops

Each stop is a good place for water, photos or simply sitting down for a while.

1

Harbour steps

The trail starts behind the fish market. Fill your bottle at the fountain.

0 km
2

Pine ridge

A short climb through pines with the first view over the bay.

2.6 km
3

Shell cove

A sheltered beach. At low tide you can walk around the rocks.

5.1 km
4

The lighthouse

Still working since 1883. The gallery opens at nine on weekends.

7.9 km
5

Salt flats

A flat path back to town past the old salt pans and herons.

12.4 km

What to bring

  • 1.5 l of water
  • Wind jacket
  • Sun hat
  • Snacks
  • Trail shoes
  • Head torch

Good to know

The cliff path between stops 2 and 3 closes in strong wind. When it does, follow the road signs inland; it adds about 1 km.
